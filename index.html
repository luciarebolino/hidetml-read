HTML: Hide Through Model Lies

Using the internet as a narrative architectural medium, built through interfaces and software that continuously construct perception and reality, this experiment questions how computational realism reshapes our understanding of the world. A test in spatializing information through the web as a dimensionless yet deep and branched site, exploring the architectural and computational dimensions of on-offline lecturing as a performative artistic medium, where the desired outputs of technologies that intuitively respond to our interactions question not what we see when we look at our screens, but what our screens hide from us.

All references and credits can be found on my Are.na channel.

To navigate the research means breaking the sense of familiarity with the platforms we occupy. Four chapters—H, T, M, and L—must be processed. Within each, an exercise in restraint through UI direction determines the conditions of flow.
